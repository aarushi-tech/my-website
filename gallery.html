Smart Technology has become a major part of our everyday lives. Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence, commonly known as AI, is one of the most exciting areas of mordern technology. It enables computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. The Internet has connected people and organisations across the world.Computer networks allow different devices to communicate and share information with each other. Cyber Security

Cyber Security focuses on protecting computers, networks, applications and digital information from online threats. As more activities move online, protecting digital information has become increasingly important. Strong passwords, secure networks, software updates and awareness of online threats are some important ways to improve digital security. Cyber security helps individuals and organisations use technology safely.
